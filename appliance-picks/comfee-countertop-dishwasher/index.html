Why choose a portable dishwasher for an apartment?

A built-in dishwasher needs a cabinet opening, a water line, a drain and usually an installer. Many apartments, rentals, dorm rooms, RVs and small kitchens have none of those. A countertop dishwasher sits next to the sink instead and plugs into a regular outlet. Because this one can run from its own water tank, it works even where you can't connect to a faucet.
