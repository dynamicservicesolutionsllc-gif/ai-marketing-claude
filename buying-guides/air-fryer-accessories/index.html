Products and practical buying considerations

TrendPlain 16 oz Glass Olive Oil Sprayer for Cooking

Getting a thin, even coat of oil on fries and vegetables is hard with a bottle, and aerosol cans add propellant and additives. This refillable glass bottle mists with a quick press and pours with a slow one, so one bottle handles both.

Who it may suit: For air fryer cooks who want a light, even coat of their own olive or avocado oil instead of aerosol spray. A quick press gives a fine mist and a slow press pours.

What to check: The lid is hand-wash only, and the filter needs an occasional rinse to keep the mist even. The 16 oz glass bottle stands on an included silicone pad.

OXO Good Grips 9-Inch Tongs with Silicone Heads

Metal tongs and forks are the fastest way to scratch a nonstick air fryer basket. These 9-inch tongs have heat-resistant silicone heads with scalloped edges, so you can flip wings or lift out vegetables without wearing down the coating.

Who it may suit: For flipping and lifting food in a nonstick air fryer basket or pan. The 9-inch length is easy to control in a small basket, and the scalloped silicone heads grip fries and wings.

What to check: Nine inches is short for a grill or deep pot, so pick longer tongs if that's the main use. They go in the dishwasher.

PINTAUR Square Silicone Air Fryer Liners, 8.5-Inch, 2-Pack

Saucy wings and marinated vegetables leave grease baked onto the basket floor. These deep square silicone pots have 2.36-inch walls and raised ribs that lift food above the drippings, then go in the dishwasher.

Who it may suit: For 5 to 6.5 quart square baskets, such as the Ninja AF141 and AF181, when you cook saucy or marinated food that would drip onto the basket.

What to check: Measure the top of your basket first: the pots measure 8.46 inches across the top and fit square baskets up to 8.9 inches. The listing says they do not fit the Cosori CAF-DC601 or Instant Vortex Plus 6-quart.
