How to Choose Insulated Drinkware

Start with your available space, intended use, cleaning requirements and the manufacturer's specifications. These products are examples to compare, not a claim that we tested or ranked them by sales.

Products and practical buying considerations

STANLEY Quencher H2.0 FlowState Tumbler, 40 oz

Holds 40 ounces with a handle and a three-position lid, and the narrow base still drops into a car cup holder. Double-wall vacuum insulation keeps ice around for hours, and it goes in the dishwasher.

Who it may suit: Suits someone who wants a large tumbler for a long day, with a handle to carry it. The narrow base still fits a car cup holder.

What to check: At 40 ounces it is heavy when full. Check your cup holder and bag. It is listed as dishwasher safe, and the lid has three positions.

Owala FreeSip Insulated Water Bottle, 24 oz

The FreeSip spout lets you sip upright through the built-in straw or tip it back and swig. A locking push-button lid keeps the spout clean, and double-wall insulation keeps water cold up to 24 hours.

Who it may suit: A good fit if you drink small amounts all day and like the choice of sipping through a straw or tipping the bottle back. The lid locks with a push button.

What to check: At 24 ounces it holds less than a large tumbler, so check whether one fill will last your day. The cold time in the listing is the manufacturer's claim.

HydroJug Traveler Insulated Bottle, 40 oz

Triple-wall insulation and a leak-resistant flip-straw lid make it a full day of water in one fill. The non-slip base fits a cup holder, and the straw detaches so everything goes in the dishwasher.

Who it may suit: Suits people who want a full day of water in one fill. It is triple-wall insulated, with a leak-resistant flip-straw lid.

What to check: At 40 ounces it is big, so check the bottle against your bag and cup holder. The straw detaches so everything can go in the dishwasher.

Owala SmoothSip Slider Coffee Tumbler, 12 oz

A slide-top lid seals completely, so coffee rides in a bag or car without splashing. Double-wall insulation keeps it hot about six hours or iced up to 24, in a 12-ounce size that fits one hand.

Who it may suit: For people who want a small coffee cup that rides in a bag or car without splashing. The slide-top lid seals completely.

What to check: At 12 ounces it is the smallest of our drinkware picks, so it will not hold a large coffee. The hot and cold times in the listing are the manufacturer's claims.

THERMOS Stainless King Travel Mug with Handle, 16 oz

Who it may suit: For people whose morning coffee has to last through a long commute. The listing says it keeps drinks hot up to 7 hours and cold up to 18.

What to check: Those times are the manufacturer's claims. The lid is leak-proof when closed and the handle helps with carrying, but check that it fits your car's cup holder.

Contigo West Loop Autoseal Travel Mug, 16 oz
