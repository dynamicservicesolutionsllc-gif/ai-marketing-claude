Products and practical buying considerations

MuellerLiving Ultra-Stick Immersion Hand Blender

Purees soup directly in the pot, so there is no ladling hot liquid into a blender in batches and no second jar to wash. The shaft detaches for the dishwasher, and the included whisk and frother attachments cover whipped cream and hot cocoa.

Who it may suit: Best for anyone who makes soup and does not want to ladle hot liquid into a blender in batches. It purees right in the pot.

What to check: The whisk and frother come with it, but our notes list no chopper or beaker. The shaft detaches for the dishwasher.

KitchenAid 5-Speed Ultra Power Hand Mixer

Handles cookie dough, mashed potatoes, and whipped cream without hauling out a stand mixer, then tucks into a drawer when the baking is done. The cord locks to either side so it stays out of the bowl, and the dishwasher-safe beaters release with one button.

Who it may suit: Handles cookie dough, mashed potatoes and whipped cream without hauling out a stand mixer. It tucks into a drawer afterward.

What to check: Hand mixers are best for lighter jobs like the ones listed, so check what you plan to make. The cord locks to either side and the beaters release with one button.

Cuisinart Mini-Prep Plus 3-Cup Food Processor

Chops onions, garlic, herbs, and nuts in a few pulses, which takes the tedious knife work out of soups, stuffing, and sauces. The reversible blade switches between chopping and grinding, and the bowl and lid are dishwasher safe, so it earns its small spot in the cabinet.

Who it may suit: For people who want to take the knife work out of onions, garlic, herbs and nuts. A few pulses do the chopping, and the reversible blade also grinds.

What to check: The bowl is 3 cups, so it suits small batches, not a large pot of soup. The bowl and lid are dishwasher safe.

Mueller Multistick 5-in-1 Immersion Blender

An 800-watt immersion blender with 12 speeds blends soups right in the pot, and the kit adds a whisk, a frother, a 20 oz beaker, and a 16 oz chopper. The detachable parts are dishwasher safe, so one compact tool covers several countertop jobs.

Who it may suit: For cooks who want one compact tool for blending, whisking, frothing and chopping. It lists 800 watts and 12 speeds plus turbo.

What to check: The kit includes a 20 oz beaker and a 16 oz chopper, so it takes more drawer space than a blender alone. The detachable parts are dishwasher safe.

Ninja Fit Compact Personal Blender

A full-size blender is a lot to haul out and wash for one smoothie. This compact 700-watt blender blends right in a 16 oz cup, then you add the spout lid and take it with you.

Who it may suit: For people who make single-serve smoothies or shakes and don't want a full-size blender taking up the counter.

What to check: The cups hold 16 oz, so it's sized for one serving at a time, and it blends only while you press down on the cup.

Magic Bullet Personal Blender, 22 oz

Big blenders are a lot to haul out for one smoothie or a bowl of salsa. The Magic Bullet uses a 250-watt base with a 22 oz cup, a short cup and a party mug, plus resealable lids so you can blend and store in the same cup.

Who it may suit: For single cooks, students and small kitchens that blend smoothies, dips and sauces in small batches.

What to check: It is a 250-watt personal blender, so it suits small batches rather than crushing big loads of ice.
