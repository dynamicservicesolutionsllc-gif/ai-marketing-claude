Before you buy, measure these

  1. Counter depth and width: The spot where the appliance will live, including space at the back for vents.
  2. Clearance above: Air fryers need open space above and around the vents; check the manual.
  3. Outlet location: Distance to the nearest outlet, without extension cords.
  4. Storage spot: Where it goes when not in use, if it won't stay out.

Buying guide: what to look for

Size to your household

A 2-quart slow cooker suits one or two people; a 6-quart air fryer suits a family batch.

Check the coating

Look at basket and crock materials and whether they're dishwasher safe.

Plan storage

Appliances that store upright or in a cabinet keep the counter clear.

Frequently asked questions

What size air fryer do I need?

Roughly 2–4 quarts for one or two people, and 5–6 quarts for a family. Check the manufacturer's serving guidance.

Is a 2-quart slow cooker big enough?

For one or two servings, dips and sides, yes.

Can I put an air fryer under kitchen cabinets?

Follow the manual's clearance guidance; most need several inches of open space around the vents. Pull it forward while it runs if space is tight.

Which small appliance should I buy first?

The one that replaces something you do most: an air fryer if you use the oven for small batches, a slow cooker if you like hands-off dinners.

Do small appliances use less electricity than an oven?

For small portions they generally heat a smaller space, so they're often more efficient. Exact use varies by model.
