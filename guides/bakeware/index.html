Buying guide: what to look for

Measure your oven first

Check the inside width and depth of your oven rack before buying sheets or large dishes. A pan that touches the walls blocks airflow and bakes unevenly.

Glass, metal or silicone

Glass holds heat for casseroles and goes from oven to table; metal sheets heat quickly for crisp cookies; silicone releases muffins easily and flexes for storage, but needs a sheet or frame under it for support.

Mind the temperature limits

Every pan lists a maximum oven temperature. Stay below it, and avoid moving glass straight from the freezer into a hot oven unless the maker says that's fine.

Cleaning and care

Nonstick lasts longer with soft sponges and no metal utensils. Let glass cool before washing, and dry silicone fully before stacking it away.

Frequently asked questions

What bakeware do I need for holiday baking?

Most home bakers get by with a deep 9x13 dish for casseroles and desserts, two or three cookie sheets, and a 12-cup muffin pan. Add specialty pans only if you bake those recipes every year.

Is a deep 9x13 dish worth it?

If you make lasagna, stuffing or layered casseroles, yes. Extra depth means fewer spillovers in the oven and more room for big holiday portions.

How do I keep pie crust edges from burning?

Bake the pie uncovered until the edges are light golden, then set a pie crust shield or a ring of foil over the rim for the rest of the bake. A reusable shield is quicker to place on a hot pie than foil strips.

Is a French rolling pin better for pie crust?

Many bakers find a tapered French pin easier to pivot when rolling dough into a circle, and it has no handles to clean around. A handled pin works fine too; choose the one that feels comfortable.

Do silicone muffin pans need greasing?

Silicone releases baked goods easily, so many recipes need little or no grease. A light spray helps with sticky batters or cheesy egg bites.

Can nonstick cookie sheets go in the dishwasher?

Some, like the NutriChef set here, are listed as dishwasher safe. Hand washing with a soft sponge still helps any nonstick coating last longer.
