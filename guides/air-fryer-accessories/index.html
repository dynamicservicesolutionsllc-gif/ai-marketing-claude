Before you buy, measure these

  1. Basket floor: Inside width and depth of the basket bottom, so liners lie flat.
  2. Basket shape: Round or square; square liners can be trimmed, round baskets need round or flexible liners.
  3. Basket depth: Tall pots and racks must leave space under the heating element.
  4. Drawer space: Where liners and tongs will live between uses.

Liners that are too big curl up the sides and block airflow. Trim or choose a smaller size.

Buying guide: what to look for

Measure the basket floor

Liners should sit flat on the bottom with a little room around the edge so hot air can still circulate. Measure the inside of the basket, not the outside of the drawer.

Reusable or disposable

Silicone liners are washed and reused; parchment liners are tossed after cooking. Many kitchens keep both: silicone for everyday batches, parchment for sticky marinades and cheese.

Use soft-tipped tools

Metal tongs and forks can scratch a nonstick basket. Silicone tips and a light mist of oil from a refillable sprayer are gentler on the coating than aerosol cans.

Cleaning and care

Let the basket cool, then soak it in warm soapy water and wipe it with a soft sponge instead of a scouring pad. Wash silicone liners after each use, empty crumbs from under the basket, and never preheat with a loose paper liner in an empty basket, because the fan can lift it into the heating element.

Frequently asked questions

Do air fryer liners affect crispiness?

A little. Liners block some airflow under the food, so perforated or slotted liners and a single layer of food help. Remove the liner for the last few minutes if you want the bottom extra crisp.

Are silicone air fryer liners safe?

Food-grade silicone liners are made for oven heat. Check the maximum temperature on the listing and stay below it; the silicone liners here list up to 446°F.

Can I put parchment paper in an air fryer?

Yes, with food on top to hold it down. Don't preheat an empty basket with a loose sheet of parchment, and follow the liner's temperature limit.

How do I clean an air fryer basket without scratching it?

Soak it in warm soapy water once it has cooled, then wipe it with a soft sponge or brush. Skip steel wool and abrasive powders, which wear down the nonstick coating.

Why use an oil sprayer instead of cooking spray?

A refillable sprayer lets you use your own oil and a light, even mist. Some aerosol sprays contain additives that can build up on nonstick coatings over time, so check your air fryer's manual.

Which accessory should I buy first?

Liners, if cleanup is what bothers you most; soft-tipped tongs, if you want to protect the basket coating.
