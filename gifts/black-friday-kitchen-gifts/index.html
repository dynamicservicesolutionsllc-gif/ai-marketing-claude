How to choose the right gift

Make your list before the sales start

Decide who you're shopping for and what they would actually use, so a big sale doesn't push you toward something that doesn't fit their kitchen.

Check the current listing on Amazon

Prices and availability change often during Black Friday week, so check the listing itself before you buy rather than relying on an old screenshot or post.

Mind the counter space

Ask, or take a look, before giving a countertop appliance. In a small kitchen a tumbler or a drawer-sized tool can be the better gift.

Order early for holiday delivery

Popular items can sell out or ship slower as December gets closer. Include a gift receipt so a color or size is easy to swap.

Frequently asked questions

What kitchen gifts are worth buying on Black Friday?

Bigger items people use every week, like an air fryer, a slow cooker or a blender, are worth putting on your list, along with everyday items like insulated tumblers.

Is an air fryer a good gift?

Yes, for someone with a spot on the counter for it. The 6-quart model here has a square basket that fits a family-size batch in a compact footprint.

What is a good kitchen gift for someone with a small kitchen?

Choose compact or multi-use items: a personal blender, a 3-cup food processor or a hand mixer stores in a cabinet or drawer instead of taking over the counter.
