Four buying checks

  1. Portion size: Decide whether you want smaller sauce portions or individual soup portions. Compare the fill lines rather than guessing from a photo.
  2. Freezer clearance: Measure a level space for the filled tray, including its lid and the clearance needed to place and remove it.
  3. Lid and storage: Compare the lid design and whether you intend to keep portions in the tray or transfer them after freezing.
  4. Care instructions: Check the exact model's cleaning instructions and permitted uses. Do not assume the lid has the same use limits as the tray.

Frequently asked questions

Is a freezer tray the same as a food-storage container?

A portioning tray creates separate portions. A larger container may suit full meals or family-size batches better. Choose for the way you actually use leftovers.

What should I measure?

Measure usable shelf width, depth and height with the drawer or door closed, then compare the exact tray and lid dimensions.

Do I need a new set of containers?

Not necessarily. Start by checking whether your existing containers already fit your portions and freezer space.
