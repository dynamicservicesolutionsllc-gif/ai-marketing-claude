How to Choose Spice Racks

Start with your available space, intended use, cleaning requirements and the manufacturer's specifications. These products are examples to compare, not a claim that we tested or ranked them by sales.

Products and practical buying considerations

HuggieGems Magnetic Spice Rack, 4-Pack

Sticks straight to the fridge — no drilling, no mounting hardware. Heavy-duty steel shelves free up real cabinet space for spices, oils, and small jars.

Who it may suit: Good for a renter or anyone who does not want to drill. The shelves stick straight to the fridge and free up cabinet space.

What to check: Magnets only grip steel, and some fridge finishes are not magnetic, so test yours with a magnet first. Check the listing for each shelf's weight limit.

Copco 3-Tier Cabinet Spice Rack, 15-Inch Non-Skid

Three staggered tiers lift the back row of spice jars and cans into view, so nothing hides behind the front row. Non-skid lining keeps jars in place, and at 15" wide by 9" deep it fits most standard cabinets with no assembly.

Who it may suit: For anyone whose back-row spices disappear behind the front row. Three staggered tiers lift every jar into view.

What to check: At 15 inches wide and 9 inches deep, measure your cabinet before ordering. It needs no assembly, and the non-skid lining keeps jars in place.

LYNK PROFESSIONAL Pull Out Spice Rack, 4-3/8 Inch Wide

Turns a narrow cabinet into a two-tier slide-out spice drawer, so jars at the back come forward instead of getting lost. It screws into the cabinet floor on full-extension glides and holds spices in their original containers, with no special jars needed.

Who it may suit: Turns a narrow cabinet into a slide-out spice drawer, so jars at the back come forward. It holds spices in their original containers.

What to check: It screws into the cabinet floor, so it is a more permanent install than a freestanding rack. It is 4-3/8 inches wide, so measure the cabinet opening and depth first.

SpaceAid Bamboo Expandable 4-Tier Spice Drawer Organizer

Spices lying flat in a drawer roll around and hide their labels, so you buy duplicates. This four-tier bamboo insert angles jars up in rows and expands from 12 to 23 inches wide to fit the drawer you have.

Who it may suit: For kitchens with a spare drawer near the stove and not enough cabinet space. It sits loose in the drawer, so renters can use it.

What to check: The listing says the drawer must be at least 3 inches deep, and jars are not included. Measure the drawer's inside width (12 to 23 inches) and the height of your jars lying down.

SONGMICS Stackable Cabinet Shelf Organizer, Set of 2

Spice jars and cans in a single row waste the empty space above them. These two wood-and-metal shelves add a second level inside a cabinet or on the counter, and the slightly different sizes let them stack, nest or sit side by side.

Who it may suit: For crowded spice cabinets, pantry shelves or a counter corner where jars and mugs need a second level.

What to check: The larger shelf is about 11.7" wide x 7.7" deep x 6.2" tall; simple assembly is required.

Before you buy
