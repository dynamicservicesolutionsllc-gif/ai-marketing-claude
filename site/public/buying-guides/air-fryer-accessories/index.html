How to Choose Air Fryer Accessories

Start with your available space, intended use, cleaning requirements and the manufacturer's specifications. These products are examples to compare, not a claim that we tested or ranked them by sales.

Products and practical buying considerations

Linda's Essentials Reusable Silicone Air Fryer Liners, 8.5-Inch, 3-Pack

Grease and stuck-on crumbs in the bottom of the basket are the main chore of air frying. These thick, perforated silicone liners catch the drips, go in the dishwasher and can be trimmed to fit, so you are not buying paper liners every week.

Who it may suit: For anyone who air fries most days and is tired of scrubbing the basket. The 8.5-inch square liners fit baskets of about 4 quarts and up, and the pack of three means one can be in the dishwasher while another is in use.

What to check: Measure your basket floor first; smaller or round baskets may need the liner trimmed. The listing rates them to 446°F, so keep cooking temperatures below that.

Reynolds Kitchens Unbleached Parchment Air Fryer Liners, 50 Count

Sticky glazes, melted cheese and marinated wings can bake onto a basket in minutes. These pre-cut unbleached parchment sheets fit most round and square baskets with no cutting, and the mess goes in the trash with the liner.

Who it may suit: For sticky or messy foods such as glazed wings, salmon and cheesy bites, and for anyone who wants zero liner cleanup. The 9-inch sheets are pre-cut to fit most round and square baskets.

What to check: They are rated up to 400°F on the air fry setting, and like any paper liner they need food on top so the fan doesn't lift them while the basket heats.

TrendPlain 16 oz Glass Olive Oil Sprayer for Cooking

Getting a thin, even coat of oil on fries and vegetables is hard with a bottle, and aerosol cans add propellant and additives. This refillable glass bottle mists with a quick press and pours with a slow one, so one bottle handles both.

Who it may suit: For air fryer cooks who want a light, even coat of their own olive or avocado oil instead of aerosol spray. A quick press gives a fine mist and a slow press pours.

What to check: The lid is hand-wash only, and the filter needs an occasional rinse to keep the mist even. The 16 oz glass bottle stands on an included silicone pad.

OXO Good Grips 9-Inch Tongs with Silicone Heads

Metal tongs and forks are the fastest way to scratch a nonstick air fryer basket. These 9-inch tongs have heat-resistant silicone heads with scalloped edges, so you can flip wings or lift out vegetables without wearing down the coating.

Who it may suit: For flipping and lifting food in a nonstick air fryer basket or pan. The 9-inch length is easy to control in a small basket, and the scalloped silicone heads grip fries and wings.

What to check: Nine inches is short for a grill or deep pot, so pick longer tongs if that's the main use. They go in the dishwasher.

PINTAUR Square Silicone Air Fryer Liners, 8.5-Inch, 2-Pack

Saucy wings and marinated vegetables leave grease baked onto the basket floor. These deep square silicone pots have 2.36-inch walls and raised ribs that lift food above the drippings, then go in the dishwasher.

Who it may suit: For 5 to 6.5 quart square baskets, such as the Ninja AF141 and AF181, when you cook saucy or marinated food that would drip onto the basket.

What to check: Measure the top of your basket first: the pots measure 8.46 inches across the top and fit square baskets up to 8.9 inches. The listing says they do not fit the Cosori CAF-DC601 or Instant Vortex Plus 6-quart.

P&P CHEF 7.5-Inch Round Cooking and Cooling Rack, 2-Pack

Food that sits flat on the basket floor can steam in its own juices instead of crisping underneath. These 7.5-inch stainless steel racks raise food 1 inch so air circulates below, and they double as steamer and cooling racks.

Who it may suit: For round air fryer baskets and stockpots with a floor wider than 7.5 inches, and for anyone who wants one rack that also works for steaming and cooling.

What to check: Measure the floor of your basket: the racks are 7.5 inches across and 1 inch tall, so leave room between the food and the heating element.

Before you buy
