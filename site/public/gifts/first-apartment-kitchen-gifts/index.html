How to choose the right gift

Cover the everyday basics first

Drying dishes, opening cans, storing leftovers. These come up in the first week, and they're easy to overlook when moving.

Size for one or two people

Big-family appliances waste space in a studio. A 2-quart slow cooker suits a first apartment better than a 6-quart one.

Keep it rental-friendly

Choose items that need no drilling or permanent installation, so nothing risks the security deposit.

Bundle small items

A can opener, a shelf liner and a sponge caddy together make a thoughtful 'kitchen starter kit'.

Frequently asked questions

What does a first apartment kitchen need?

A dish rack, can opener, food storage containers, basic cookware and utensils, and a simple way to cook a meal, like a slow cooker or kettle.

What is a good housewarming gift for a small apartment?

Something useful that doesn't take much space: a compact dish rack, an electric kettle or a drawer organizer.

Are slow cookers good for one person?

A small 2-quart slow cooker is a good fit; it cooks a few servings without leaving the pot half empty.
