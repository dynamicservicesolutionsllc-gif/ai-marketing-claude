Buying guide: what to look for

Countertop or over the sink

Countertop racks hold more upright plates; roll-up racks free the counter but need a sink that matches their width.

Check the drainage

A drainboard or a rack over the basin keeps puddles off the counter.

Measure the sink

For over-the-sink racks, measure the shorter side of the sink opening.

Frequently asked questions

What dish rack takes up the least counter space?

An over-the-sink roll-up rack uses no counter space at all, as long as it fits your sink.

Is a roll-up rack strong enough for pots?

Check the stated weight capacity; steel-core racks generally handle cookware.
