Buying guide: what to look for

Seal what goes stale

Use latching or gasket lids for flour, sugar, rice and cereal.

Use the depth

Bins with handles and turntables bring items at the back of deep shelves to the front.

Stack to the shelf height

Measure shelf clearance so stacked containers or tiered can racks fit.

Frequently asked questions

Are airtight containers worth it for a pantry?

For dry goods that go stale or attract pests, yes. Clear airtight containers also show when you're running low.

How do I organize a deep pantry shelf?

Use pull-out bins or a lazy Susan so items at the back are reachable without unloading the front.
