Frequently asked questions

Is an immersion blender as good as a countertop blender?

For soups and sauces it is more convenient; for crushed ice and thick smoothies a countertop blender is stronger.

Do I need a hand mixer or a stand mixer?

A hand mixer covers most home baking; a stand mixer suits frequent bread or large batches.
