Before you buy, measure these

  1. Cabinet interior width and depth: So a tiered rack fits with the door closed, or a pull-out rack clears the opening and hinges.
  2. Shelf height: Jar height plus the top tier's height must clear the shelf above.
  3. Your jar sizes: Diameter and height of your most common jars.
  4. Fridge side: Clear space beside the fridge, and whether a magnet sticks to it.

Count your jars before buying. A rack that holds fewer jars than you own just moves the clutter.

Buying guide: what to look for

Inside or outside the cabinet

Tiered risers organize an existing cabinet; magnetic racks add new storage on the fridge.

Measure the cabinet

Check width and depth before ordering a tiered rack.

Mind the heat

Keep spices away from the stove and direct sun so they last longer.

Frequently asked questions

Do magnetic spice racks damage the fridge?

Most use rubber-backed magnets; check the listing and keep the surface clean before attaching.

How many spice jars fit on a 3-tier rack?

It depends on jar size; compare the rack's width and depth with the jars you own.

Will a magnetic spice rack work on a stainless steel fridge?

Only if the panel is magnetic. Test with a fridge magnet before buying; many stainless doors are, but not all.

Where should spices be stored?

Away from the stove's heat and direct sun. Close by is convenient, but right above the burners shortens shelf life.

How do I organize spices so I can find them?

Alphabetize, or group by cuisine, and keep the ones you use weekly on the front tier.
